原文完整摘要

Frontier Vision-Language Models (VLMs) such as GPT-6 Astra have demonstrated remarkable capabilities in robot control, suggesting that broad pretraining provides knowledge valuable for physical action. Yet most Vision-Language-Action (VLA) policies still rely on much smaller VLM backbones, leaving this potential largely untapped. We ask whether frontier-scale representations can provide a stronger foundation for robot learning. We introduce ScaleVLA, which connects a 552B DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash backbone to a trainable action DiT, and systematically compare VLMs from 2B to 552B under a shared training setup. We evaluate three fundamental capabilities of robot foundation models. In sample efficiency, just 10 demonstrations outperform a 2B backbone trained with 50 (87.8% vs. 85.6%). In generalization, LIBERO-PRO success reaches 45.1%, compared with at most 31.3% for smaller backbones. In precise manipulation, real-world charger insertion improves from 4/10 to 9/10 successes, with gains concentrated in contact-sensitive alignment. Despite its scale, ScaleVLA achieves 216 ms median latency per action chunk. Our findings suggest that frontier VLMs can serve not only as reasoning agents, but also as powerful representational foundations for efficient and generalizable robot policies.

原文完整摘要

Diarization-conditioned target-speaker ASR (TS-ASR), in particular Diarization-conditioned Whisper (DiCoW), is an accurate and data-efficient approach to multi-talker transcription. It has, however, three structural limitations: the model transcribes each speaker independently, operates on short (30 s) windows, and optimizes its diarization and TS-ASR components separately. We address the first two. We integrate the diarization conditioning into a longform Parakeet backbone (DiCoP) and add cross-speaker attention, which lets the model attend across the speaker dimension so that speakers are no longer decoded in isolation, and an STNO mask refiner that re-estimates the diarization-derived conditioning. As conversational data with genuine multi-speaker interaction is scarce, we scale training with a pseudo-labeling pipeline that mines 5.4k hours of in-the-wild multi-speaker audio from YODAS2. Combining these with synthetic and real data yields state-of-the-art results across several meeting benchmarks. We open-source the resulting models, DiCoP and Pandemonium, and the pseudo-labeled data.

原文完整摘要

Fine-grained questions about long videos ask how many times an action occurs, in what order events unfold, or exactly when a state changes. Answering them can require finding brief moments scattered across hours of footage. Agentic models that decide when and where to look promise to find these moments, yet this ability is hard both to measure and to learn. Sparse supporting evidence may establish that an action occurred without establishing its total count; verifying timeline-wide claims requires checking for missed occurrences and double counting. Answer-only supervision, in turn, does not explicitly supervise the search for evidence. We present NexSight, which builds verifiable tasks to both evaluate and train agentic long-video understanding. Its agentic pipeline, SightForge, accepts a task only if a verifier blind to the draft answer can reconstruct it from evidence densely reacquired from the raw video and counterexample checks fail to refute it. This yields SightLine, 310 evidence-traceable tasks from 108 long videos, evaluated through SightLoop, a shared multiscale harness supporting temporal exploration, local inspection, evidence integration, and revisiting while recording returned observations. The strongest model reaches only 54.52% on SightLine, yet 79.35% when given the annotated evidence. 原文完整摘要

Full-duplex spoken dialogue models learn turn-taking, overlaps and backchannels from conversations recorded with one channel per speaker, but most available conversational audio is a single mono mix. We present Kezek, a model that turns a mono recording of exactly two people into one track per person with the original length and timing; overlaps and short replies ("yeah", "mm-hm") stay on the track of the person who said them. Kezek decides who speaks when and who is who once for the whole recording (stage 1: a two-voice activity model guided by a speaker fingerprint trained to be insensitive to phase), and a conditioned time-domain separator only carries out that decision (stage 2), with outputs that always sum to the input. Standard separation metrics miss the failures that matter for dialogue data, so we report short-reply retention, leakage onto the other person's solo, channel swaps and coverage. Against the open pipeline pyannote -> DialogueSidon on the same recordings, Kezek keeps more short replies (0.83 vs. 0.69 on Open Yap test; 0.80 vs. 0.71 over the other's speech on unseen telephone speech from CallHome English), mostly because it returns the whole recording while the external pipeline drops part of it (on the audio both return, reply retention is about the same: 0.859 vs. 0.865 on Open Yap); it also leaks less, swaps channels less often, and runs 4-5 times faster (about 230 hours of audio per GPU-hour). We also report negative results and an ideal-mask ceiling that locate the remaining gap: quiet replies under a louder voice. Code and weights are released under permissive licences.

原文完整摘要

Video understanding, particularly with accompanying audio, remains a central challenge in multimodal intelligence. In this report, we introduce \textbf{WeMM-Video}, a family of Chinese-English bilingual omni-modal foundation models spanning 2B, 4B, 9B, and 35B-A3B variants. Centering on joint audio-visual perception and reasoning, WeMM-Video unifies text, image, audio, and video processing within a hybrid-thinking paradigm, seamlessly supporting both a non-thinking instruct mode for fast, direct responses and a thinking mode for explicit step-by-step reasoning. The models are trained on a large-scale multimodal corpus of approximately 1.2 trillion tokens through progressive pre-training and a multi-stage post-training pipeline incorporating multi-teacher OPD, anti-repetition MrDPO, and specialist RL. Evaluated across more than 120 benchmarks, WeMM-Video demonstrates superior performance in omni-modal video understanding and reasoning, while maintaining highly competitive capabilities across general audio, silent video, image, and text tasks. Specifically, in omni-modal video evaluations, our flagship WeMM-Video-35B-A3B establishes a new open-source state of the art, substantially outperforming proprietary models such as Qwen3.5-Omni-Flash and Seed2.0-Mini-0428 while narrowing the gap with the strong Gemini-3.0-Flash, and our compact WeMM-Video-9B surpasses the larger open-source Qwen3-Omni-30B-A3B-Instruct. We publicly release the WeMM-Video family along with our evaluation and reproduction pipeline to foster further research and development in the open-source community.

VoxMel TTS 技术报告
VoxMel TTS Technical Report

AI 摘要连续自回归 TTS 依赖于声学目标，该目标能够实现稳定的下一个潜在模型，同时保留细粒度的声学细节。虽然现有方法采用学习的压缩表示（例如，连续 VAE 潜伏）来使预测易于处理，但它们依赖于复杂的神经编码器，而这通常会限制生成保真度。相反，像梅尔谱图这样的免训练目标消除了编码器开销，但它们自回归生成的潜力在很大程度上仍未得到充分开发。在这项工作中，我们提出了 VoxMel，一种用于自回归 TTS 的无标记器框架，它通过固定的、精确可逆的两轴 Haar 变换使稳定的 mel 建模变得易于处理。该变换首先将每个梅尔频谱图沿频率分解为局部平均值和频率残差，然后将平均分量沿时间分解为粗梅尔和时间残差。具有流匹配头的语言模型主干会自动生成 Coarse-Mel。然后，非自回归残差层根据粗梅尔预测时间残差，然后根据重建的频率平均梅尔预测频率残差。两个残差都不会反馈到自回归历史中。

原文完整摘要

Continuous autoregressive TTS relies on an acoustic target that enables stable next-latent modeling while preserving fine-grained acoustic details. While existing approaches employ learned compressed representations (e.g., continuous VAE latents) to render prediction tractable, they rely on complex neural encoders that often cap generation fidelity. Conversely, training-free targets like mel-spectrograms eliminate encoder overhead, yet their potential for autoregressive generation remains largely underexplored. In this work, we propose VoxMel, a tokenizer-free framework for autoregressive TTS that makes stable mel modeling tractable through a fixed, exactly invertible two-axis Haar transform. The transform first decomposes each mel spectrogram along frequency into local averages and a frequency residual, then splits the averaged component along time into Coarse-Mel and a temporal residual. A language-model backbone with a flow-matching head autoregressively generates Coarse-Mel. Non-autoregressive residual tiers then predict the temporal residual from Coarse-Mel, followed by the frequency residual conditioned on the reconstructed Frequency-Averaged Mel. Neither residual is fed back into the autoregressive history. Trained on 100K hours of Emilia, the supervised 0.6B model obtains 1.51\% WER and 0.738 speaker similarity (SIM) on test-en, and 1.29\% CER and 0.755 SIM on test-zh. GRPO improves these results to 1.15\% WER / 0.753 SIM and 1.17\% CER / 0.776 SIM, respectively. We also distill the guided flow head into two- and four-step students, requiring 2 or 4 head evaluations per coarse frame. The four-step student retains competitive SIM and nearly matches the supervised model in WER/CER.

Despite recent advances in large vision-language models (LVLMs) for video-related tasks, automatic rough-cut remains underexplored, without a comprehensive benchmark for evaluation or mature workflows for producing executable cut plans. Experiments show that current LVLMs remain limited in producing expert-aligned cut plans. Such systems must be proactive for natural and useful human interaction: they should respond as soon as sufficient evidence becomes available while avoiding premature, redundant, or incorrect responses. Our experiments result in two models, with our BLIP-Live-Qwen model achieving state-of-the-art performance across four proactive streaming benchmarks, with gains up to 44% over baselines and outperforming multiple closed API models. Our code and data will be released publicly.

PIPETTE：快速注射 Purple-Teaming 打造强大的 LLM 代理
PIPETTE: Prompt Injection Purple-Teaming for Robust LLM Agents

AI 摘要大语言模型（LLM） 代理经常与不受信任的外部数据交互，使它们遭受间接提示注入 (IPI) 攻击，这些攻击会劫持代理来执行攻击者选择的任务。为了大规模发现这些漏洞，自动化红队方法通常利用强化学习 (RL) 来生成实现高攻击成功率 (ASR) 的攻击。模型开发人员利用内部红队期间发现的攻击，通过部署前的对抗性训练来强化代理，这是一种在网络安全中称为紫队的协作攻防过程。然而，我们表明，在红队期间实现高 ASR 并不能保证后续对抗训练的鲁棒性增益。具体来说，我们发现两种最先进的基于 RL 的红队方法 RL-Hammer 和 PISmith 遭受模式崩溃的困扰，反复发现类似的攻击策略，这些策略的额外成功并没有为对抗性训练带来额外的鲁棒性改进。为了解决这一差距，我们引入了 PIPETTE，这是一个统一的即时注入紫队框架，可以有效地将红队发现转化为防御者稳健性的改进。

原文完整摘要

Large language model (LLM) agents routinely interact with untrusted external data, exposing them to indirect prompt injection (IPI) attacks that hijack agents into executing attacker-chosen tasks. To uncover these vulnerabilities at scale, automated red-teaming methods often leverage reinforcement learning (RL) to generate attacks that achieve high attack success rates (ASR). Model developers use the attacks discovered during internal red-teaming to harden agents through adversarial training before deployment, a collaborative offensive-defensive process known in cybersecurity as purple-teaming. However, we show that achieving high ASR during red-teaming does not guarantee robustness gains from subsequent adversarial training. Specifically, we find that two state-of-the-art RL-based red-teaming methods, RL-Hammer and PISmith, suffer from mode collapse, repeatedly discovering similar attack strategies whose additional successes add no additional robustness improvements to adversarial training. To address this gap, we introduce PIPETTE, a unified prompt injection purple-teaming framework that effectively converts red-teaming discoveries into defender robustness improvements. PIPETTE employs a dictionary learning-based reward that encourages broader coverage of attack strategies during red-teaming. It also integrates an on-policy adversarial training pipeline tailored to IPI defense, enabling agents to resist attacks while completing legitimate user tasks. Extensive evaluations across agentic benchmarks (AgentDojo and ASB) demonstrate that PIPETTE produces defenders that achieve near-zero attack success rates on static attacks and can withstand strong adaptive attacks, while maintaining benign task utility close to that of the undefended baseline.

ALIVE：使流媒体视频模型具有交互性
ALIVE: Making Streaming Video Models Interactive

AI 摘要流媒体视频LLM可以持续观察世界，但通常会单独处理用户请求。我们推出了 ALIVE，一种交互式流媒体视频模型，支持主动监控、流媒体任务协调和反馈寻求交互。我们构建 ALIVE-SFT-288K 和 ALIVE-RL-3K 进行两阶段训练。对比监督微调教导何时以及如何做出反应，而使用行为一致信用分配 (BACA) 的强化学习结合了轨迹级别和局部优势，以改进多轮交互中的决策、响应和沉默。我们还引入 FSI-Bench 来评估反馈寻求和后续任务完成情况。 ALIVE 在四个公共基准和 FSI-Bench 上的比较模型中获得了最高的总体得分，展示了强大的流媒体理解和交互能力。

原文完整摘要

Streaming video LLMs can continuously observe the world, yet often handle user requests in isolation. We introduce ALIVE, an interactive streaming video model supporting proactive monitoring, streaming task coordination, and feedback-seeking interaction. We construct ALIVE-SFT-288K and ALIVE-RL-3K for two-stage training. Contrastive supervised fine-tuning teaches when and how to respond, while reinforcement learning with Behavior-Aligned Credit Assignment (BACA) combines trajectory-level and local advantages to refine decisions, responses, and silence across multi-turn interactions. We also introduce FSI-Bench to evaluate feedback seeking and subsequent task completion. ALIVE achieves the highest overall scores among the compared models on four public benchmarks and FSI-Bench, demonstrating strong streaming understanding and interactive capabilities.

TCP4D：用于机器人操纵的统一工具中心点跟踪和生成模型
TCP4D: A Unified Tool-Center-Point Tracking and Generation Model for Robot Manipulation

AI 摘要机器人操作视频提供了丰富的视觉体验，但从 RGB 观察中恢复机器人动作和抓手状态注释仍然具有挑战性。使用与实施例无关的工具中心点（TCP）姿势作为动作空间，我们提出了 TCP4D，这是一个统一的模型，其中共享的 4D 场景表示既跟踪观察到的 TCP 运动并生成未来的 TCP 轨迹。给定单目视频和初始 2D TCP 查询，跟踪器直接恢复 SE(3) 中的度量 TCP 位姿和夹具状态以及场景几何和相机参数。生成器沿着恢复的 TCP 轨迹从相同的 4D 表示中采样局部特征，然后将它们与机器人观察和语言指令相结合以预测未来的 TCP 状态。我们新的跨实施例增强管道通过重新定位恢复的运动并合成相应的观察结果，将现有的 RGB 视频转换为替代实施例的演示，而无需记录源机器人状态。实验表明，跟踪器在 DROID 和 RoboLab 上实现了 7.60 和 16.07 mm 的绝对轨迹误差，并且仅经过 10 次演示就适应了新的实施例。

原文完整摘要

Robot manipulation videos provide a rich visual experience, yet recovering robot action and gripper-state annotations from RGB observations still remains challenging. Using the embodiment-agnostic tool-center-point (TCP) pose as the action space, we present TCP4D, a unified model in which a shared 4D scene representation both tracks observed TCP motion and generates future TCP trajectories. Given a monocular video and initial 2D TCP queries, the tracker directly recovers metric TCP poses in SE(3) and gripper states alongside scene geometry and camera parameters. The generator samples local features from the same 4D representation along the recovered TCP tracks, then combines them with robot observations and language instructions to predict future TCP states. Our new cross-embodiment augmentation pipeline converts existing RGB videos into demonstrations for alternative embodiments by retargeting the recovered motion and synthesizing the corresponding observations, without recorded source-robot states. Experiments show that the tracker achieves absolute trajectory errors of 7.60 and 16.07 mm on DROID and RoboLab and adapts to a new embodiment with only ten demonstrations. The generator achieves 69.19% mean success on RoboTwin and outperforms well-established policy models on two real-world tasks, while joint training on source and augmented demonstrations improves target-robot success by 41.5% over augmented-only training.

HINT-WAM：世界行动模式的事后知情培训
HINT-WAM: Hindsight-Informed Training for World Action Models

AI 摘要通过将动作生成与未来视觉动态的预测相结合，世界动作模型（WAM）在机器人操作方面显示出了有希望的泛化性和数据效率。然而，将这些能力转化为可靠的任务执行对于现有的训练后方法来说仍然是一个挑战。离线监督微调提供了对专家轨迹的密集监督，但其流匹配动作专家仅接受噪声演示动作的训练，而从未接受其自身采样器产生的中间状态的训练；针对特定任务的适应也会损害预先训练的能力。策略强化学习根据策略自身的行为进行训练，但稀疏的任务奖励使探索和信用分配变得困难，而密集的奖励塑造仍然提供标量评估而不是直接的行动目标。我们引入 \textbf{HINT-WAM}，这是第一个策略自蒸馏框架，它使用事后诸葛亮（未来的演示视频）来监督动作专家在其自己的采样轨迹上，作为 WAM 本身可用的特权信息。

原文完整摘要

World action models (WAMs) have shown promising generalization and data efficiency in robot manipulation by coupling action generation with predictions of future visual dynamics. Translating these capabilities into reliable task execution, however, remains a challenge for existing post-training methods. Offline supervised fine-tuning provides dense supervision on expert trajectories, but its flow-matching action expert is trained only on noised demonstration actions and never on the intermediate states produced by its own sampler; task-specific adaptation can also compromise pretrained capabilities. On-policy reinforcement learning trains on the policy's own behavior, yet sparse task rewards make exploration and credit assignment difficult, while dense reward shaping still provides scalar evaluations rather than direct action targets. We introduce \textbf{HINT-WAM}, the first on-policy self-distillation framework that supervises the action expert on its own sampling trajectories using hindsight, the future video of the demonstration, as privileged information available to the WAM itself. For each demonstration observation, the student generates its action through its own denoising trajectory, and at the states along this trajectory the same model acts as a teacher conditioned additionally on this hindsight, providing a conditional action distribution as a dense training target, while the student sees only deployment-time inputs and needs no environmental rewards or simulator interaction. To transfer this supervision to a flow-matching action expert, HINT-WAM distills the teacher into the student with an energy score, a distribution-level objective that matches the teacher's action distribution from the student's own samples rather than regressing its velocity field. The project web is available in https://tuandattt.github.io/hintwam/.

PROLA：预测时空天气降尺度的主正交低阶适应
PROLA: Principal-Orthogonal Low-rank Adaptation for Predictive Spatiotemporal Weather Downscaling

AI 摘要高分辨率天气预报对于解决粗略的全球预报所遗漏的当地大气模式非常重要。大型预训练天气主干网改善了全球预报，但将其调整为从最近的粗略状态生成未来高分辨率场仍然具有挑战性且成本高昂。我们将这种设置表述为预测时空天气降尺度（PSWD），这项任务可以联合细化空间和时间分辨率，同时预测未来的高分辨率轨迹。为此，我们提出了一个框架，使用共享的预训练主干、固定的数值支架和轻量级残差头将预测分解为空间和时间阶段。对于骨干适应，我们引入了 PROLA（PRincipal-Orthogonal Low-rank Adaptation），它在预训练权重的主方向与其正交补集之间分配固定的低秩预算。 PROLA 使用梯度信号进一步重新调整排名 1 优化器更新，在不增加可训练排名的情况下提高适应性。 PSWD 设置中的实验表明 PROLA 优于代表性的低秩适应基线。

原文完整摘要

High-resolution weather prediction is important for resolving local atmospheric patterns missed by coarse global forecasts. Large pretrained weather backbones have improved global forecasting, but adapting them to produce future high-resolution fields from recent coarse states remains challenging and costly. We formulate this setting as Predictive Spatiotemporal Weather Downscaling (PSWD), a task that jointly refines spatial and temporal resolution while predicting future high-resolution trajectories. To this end, we propose a framework that decomposes prediction into spatial and temporal stages using a shared pretrained backbone, fixed numerical scaffolds, and lightweight residual heads. For backbone adaptation, we introduce PROLA (PRincipal-Orthogonal Low-rank Adaptation), which allocates a fixed low-rank budget between pretrained weights' principal directions and their orthogonal complement. PROLA further rescales rank-1 optimizer updates using gradient signals, improving adaptation without increasing trainable rank. Experiments in the PSWD setting show that PROLA outperforms representative low-rank adaptation baselines. Further experiments across diverse datasets and tasks support the broader applicability of PROLA, showing that its benefits extend beyond the primary weather downscaling setting.

WAM-Policy：具有四足机器人操作的策略蒸馏的世界行动模型
WAM-Policy: World Action Models with On-Policy Distillation for Quadrupedal Loco-Manipulation

AI 摘要无臂四足动物可以进行局部操作，即用三条腿行走，同时用前腿进行操作，但现有的政策没有遵循语言指令。世界行动模型（WAM）已成为有效的语言条件操纵策略，但针对机器人手臂进行了预训练，采样速度缓慢。 WAM-Policy 通过两个阶段将一个 WAM 转变为四足机器人操纵策略。第一个是跨形态策略适应和加速，将以手臂为中心的 WAM 应用于四足动物，并通过策略自蒸馏 (OPSD) 将转移的策略通过一个视频和两个动作步骤提炼为学生。然而，这个学生却受到老师的限制，老师只是模仿示范。因此，第二阶段“特定任务的改进和合并”通过强化学习（RL）来调整教师，并通过多教师的策略蒸馏（M-OPD）将速度慢的专家合并为一个几步的学生。在 QLM-Bench 上，在没有远程操作的情况下合成了 10 个模拟任务，1000 次演示，迁移策略达到 77.0%，OPSD 学生保持 76.7%，同时采样速度提高了 14 倍。

原文完整摘要

Arm-free quadrupeds can perform loco-manipulation, walking on three legs while manipulating with a front leg, yet no existing policy for it follows language instructions. World action models (WAMs) have emerged as effective language-conditioned manipulation policies, but are pretrained for robot arms and sample slowly. WAM-Policy turns one WAM into a quadrupedal loco-manipulation policy via two stages. The first, Cross-Morphology Policy Adaptation and Acceleration, adapts the arm-centric WAM to quadrupeds and distills the transferred policy into a student with one video and two action steps by on-policy self-distillation (OPSD). This student, however, is bounded by its teacher, which only imitates demonstrations. The second stage, Task-Specific Improvement and Merging, therefore tunes the teacher by reinforcement learning (RL) and merges the slow specialists into one few-step student by multi-teacher on-policy distillation (M-OPD). On QLM-Bench, ten simulated tasks with 1,000 demonstrations synthesized without teleoperation, the transferred policy reaches 77.0%, and the OPSD student keeps 76.7% while sampling 14 times faster. Task-specific RL and M-OPD lift it to 85.0%. We also deploy the OPSD student on a real Unitree Go2 Air. WAM-Policy thus achieves a stronger quadrupedal policy at a fraction of the inference cost.

从存储库到训练环境：通过环境演化循环扩展长视野编码代理
From Repositories to Training Environments: Scaling Long-Horizon Coding Agents with Environment Evolution Loop

AI 摘要为长期任务训练编码代理需要具有可验证反馈的环境。现有的管道通常遵循线性过程，以验证结束，建立可用性，但在提高培训价值方面几乎没有提供指导。递归自我改进（RSI）通常需要模型部署或模型训练，然后进行下游评估，长期任务的成本和反馈延迟会放大。我们如何使用及时、可靠的代理信号来提高训练价值并大规模合成环境？我们推出了 RepoFactory，这是一个自动化管道，可将真实存储库转换为可验证的环境，在给定语言规范的情况下，代理从头开始构建完整的项目。其环境演化循环通过代理反馈优化了结合有效性、结构耦合和实施范围的乘法目标。 RepoFactory 生成跨 17 个软件领域的 10,159 个环境。

原文完整摘要

Training coding agents for long-horizon tasks requires environments with verifiable feedback. Existing pipelines often follow a linear process that ends with validation, establishing usability but providing little guidance on improving training value. Recursive self-improvement (RSI) often requires model rollouts or model training followed by downstream evaluation, with costs and feedback delays amplified on long-horizon tasks. How can we use timely, reliable proxy signals to improve training value and synthesize environments at scale? We present RepoFactory, an automated pipeline that converts real repositories into verifiable environments where agents build complete projects from scratch given a language specification. Its Environment Evolution Loop optimizes a multiplicative objective combining validity, structural coupling, and implementation scope through proxy feedback. RepoFactory produces 10,159 environments across 17 software domains. Training Qwen3.5-35B-A3B on these environments yields RepoHorizen-35B-A3B, which achieves gains of 22.6 percentage points on NL2Repo-Bench and 8.5 points on average across five benchmarks covering long-horizon coding and agentic tasks in real-world production settings, compared with 17.5 and 6.4 points without environment evolution. These results highlight the training value of RepoFactory environments and the additional gains from the Environment Evolution Loop.

了解动作会改变什么：动态忠实世界模型的动作效果匹配
Learning What Actions Change: Action Effect Matching for Dynamics-Faithful World Models

AI 摘要世界模型旨在预测行动下的环境动态，从而无需在现实世界中执行即可预测行动后果。然而，尽管制作的视频越来越真实，当前的世界模型往往无法捕捉微小的动作变化如何影响未来的观察。这限制了它们在机器人政策评估和基于模型的规划等任务中的使用，在这些任务中，几乎相同的行动可能会导致性质不同的结果。为了解决这个问题，我们提出了 AFFECT，一种辅助训练目标，用于学习动作扰动引起的视觉变化的分布。具体来说，我们将时间相关的噪声注入到受任务无关的人类远程操作训练的策略的动作中。然后，我们训练世界模型，使其生成的视频像录制的视频一样对这种噪声做出响应，而不需要从相同的初始状态重复执行。在 OGBench 模拟和现实世界的 DROID 和 ALOHA-2 设置中，AFFECT 使世界模型的推出更加忠实于真实结果。

原文完整摘要

World models aim to predict environment dynamics under actions, enabling the prediction of action consequences without executing them in the real world. Yet despite producing increasingly realistic videos, current world models often fail to capture how small action changes affect future observations. This limits their use in tasks such as robotic policy evaluation and model-based planning, where nearly identical actions can lead to qualitatively different outcomes. To address this, we propose AFFECT, an auxiliary training objective for learning the distribution of visual changes induced by action perturbations. Specifically, we inject temporally correlated noise into the actions of a policy trained on task-agnostic human teleoperation. We then train the world model so that its generated videos respond to this noise as the recorded videos do, without requiring repeated executions from the same initial state. Across OGBench simulation and real-world DROID and ALOHA-2 setups, AFFECT makes world-model rollouts more faithful to real outcomes. On DROID, the Pearson correlation between policy success rates in the world model and on the real robot rises from 0.47 to 0.79, alongside up to 23% lower FVD. These gains carry over to planning, where selecting actions with AFFECT-trained world models improves task success by up to 8.3%.

通过动态路由、以 KV 为中心的执行和运行时流水线加速块注意力的混合
Accelerating Mixture of Block Attention via Dynamic Routing, KV-Centric Execution and Runtime Pipelining

AI 摘要块注意力混合（MoBA）通过为每个查询选择键值（KV）块来减少长上下文计算。 FlashMoBA 高效地实现了 MoBA，但保留了固定的 Top-k 预算和以查询为中心的时间表。固定 Top-k 在不同的分数分布上应用一项预算，并将路由与任务粒度结合起来；当多个范围选择同一列时，查询范围所有权片段 K/V 重用。我们提出了 LightningMoBA，它通过动态路由、以 KV 为中心的所有权和运行时管道来共同设计选择和执行。动态路由阈值 MoBA 的每个查询块分数并直接发出可变基数稀疏元数据。以 KV 为中心的所有权按（查询头、KV 块）列对选定的行进行分组，将高扇出列拆分为有界任务，并重用每个任务加载的 KV 块。在 Hopper 上，流水线与 Tensor Core 计算重叠常规 K/V 传输和不规则查询收集。这些机制共同将保留与 GPU 调度粒度脱钩。

原文完整摘要

Mixture of Block Attention (MoBA) reduces long-context computation by selecting key-value (KV) blocks for each query. FlashMoBA implements MoBA efficiently but retains a fixed Top-k budget and query-centric schedule. Fixed Top-k applies one budget across different score distributions and couples routing to task granularity; query-range ownership fragments K/V reuse when several ranges select the same column. We present LightningMoBA, which co-designs selection and execution through dynamic routing, KV-centric ownership, and runtime pipelining. Dynamic routing thresholds MoBA's per-query block scores and directly emits variable-cardinality sparse metadata. KV-centric ownership groups selected rows by (query head, KV block) column, splits high-fanout columns into bounded tasks, and reuses each task's loaded KV tile. On Hopper, pipelining overlaps regular K/V transfers and irregular query gathers with Tensor Core computation. Together, these mechanisms decouple retention from GPU scheduling granularity. At matched Top-16 retention across five checkpoints, LightningMoBA accelerates end-to-end generation by 1.20 to 1.60x while raising mean RULER score from 74.42 to 75.28.
